  <h1 style="font-size: 1.8rem; margin: 0.75rem 0 0.5rem;">
    Ecofeminist Approaches in Dystopian Narratives: The Domination of Nature and Women
  </h1>
  <p style="color: var(--accent); font-weight: 600; text-transform: uppercase; font-size: 0.82rem; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
    Aldous Huxley's <em>Brave New World</em> • Theoretical Framework: Karen J. Warren
  </p>

  <div class="abstract-quote">
    <strong>Abstract:</strong> This article explores the intersection of patriarchal control and environmental exploitation in Aldous Huxley's <em>Brave New World</em> through an ecofeminist lens. As a foundational dystopian text, the novel presents a hyper-industrialized society where natural processes are entirely suppressed. Drawing upon Karen J. Warren's concept of the "logic of domination" and value dualisms, the study examines how the World State establishes absolute authoritarian control by subjugating both the female body and the natural world.
  </div>

  <div class="abstract-quote" style="border-left-color: var(--primary); background: #f8fafc;">
    <strong>Öz:</strong> Bu makale, Aldous Huxley'nin <em>Cesur Yeni Dünya</em> adlı eserindeki ataerkil kontrol ve çevresel sömürünün kesişimini eko feminist bir çerçeveden incelemektedir. Distopya romanlarının ilk eserlerinden olan roman, doğal süreçlerin tamamen baskılandığı aşırı sanayileşmiş bir toplum sunmaktadır. Karen J. Warren'ın "tahakküm mantığı" ve değer ikilikleri kavramlarından yola çıkan bu çalışma, Dünya Devleti'nin hem kadın bedenini hem de doğal dünyayı tahakküm altına alarak nasıl mutlak bir otoriter kontrol kurduğunu araştırmaktadır.
  </div>

  <h3 style="margin-top: 1.75rem;">Core Analytical Pillars</h3>
  <ul style="margin: 0.75rem 0 1.25rem 1.4rem;">
    <li><strong>Technological Hegemony:</strong> The eradication of biological maternity via the Bokanovsky Process as an institutional victory of mechanized culture over nature.</li>
    <li><strong>Bodily Reduction:</strong> Social conditioning of female subjectivity into commodified, utilitarian "pneumatic" objects.</li>
    <li><strong>Soma and Emotional Pacification:</strong> Compulsory pharmacological intervention suppressing critical consciousness and organic grief.</li>
  </ul>
